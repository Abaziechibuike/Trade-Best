# TRADE BEST

One-line summary
-- A simple, mobile-friendly marketplace that helps local traders list, advertise, and ship their goods online so customers can discover and buy from them without needing to visit the market in person.

Problem
-- Many traders and small shop owners rely on foot traffic and loud hawking or banners to find customers. This limits reach, causes long waiting times, and reduces sales opportunities, especially for buyers who live far away.

Target users
-- Local traders, shop owners, market vendors, and small business people who want a low-friction way to reach more customers.

Observed context
-- Markets and business districts where vendors depend on in-person customers and local advertising.

Current solutions & shortcomings
-- Tactics: banners, shouting/hawking, word-of-mouth.
-- Shortcomings: limited geographic reach, manual effort, unpredictable foot traffic, and no easy way to serve distant customers.

Proposed solution
-- An online platform to list goods, advertise promotions, and arrange shipping or local delivery (waybills) so buyers from anywhere can discover and purchase items.

Minimum Viable Product (MVP) — core features
-- Vendor onboarding: quick signup and simple shop profile.
-- Product listings: add photos, descriptions, prices, and stock status.
-- Search & discovery: browse by category, keyword, and location.
-- Basic orders: customers place orders and receive order confirmation.
-- Shipping support: create waybills or arrange local pickup/delivery.
-- Simple dashboard: sales overview and order management for vendors.

Future improvements (after MVP)
-- Payments integration, ratings and reviews, ads/promoted listings, inventory sync, analytics, and multi-language support.

Technical approach (starter)
-- Web app (responsive) with a lightweight backend and a simple database. Start with a single-page listing flow and expand iteratively.
